Scope

Services

Award and eligibility

  • Pre-award and post-award bid protests at the agency level and at GAO
  • Size and status protests, filed or defended, before the SBA area office and OHA
  • VetCert applications, recertification and eligibility review under 13 C.F.R. Part 128
  • SDVOSB joint ventures and teaming agreements, including the 40 percent work requirement

Performance and payment

  • Requests for equitable adjustment for changes, delay, disruption and differing site conditions
  • Certified claims and contracting officer final decisions under the Contract Disputes Act
  • Appeals to the Armed Services and Civilian Boards of Contract Appeals
  • Cure notices, show cause letters and terminations for default or convenience

Engagement terms

  • Conflicts are cleared before the firm reviews confidential information.
  • Scope and fee are set out in a written engagement letter before work begins.
  • Filing fees and costs, including the $500 GAO filing fee, are billed at cost.
  • The same attorney handles the matter from intake through decision.

Before contacting the firm

  • Note the date and time of the debriefing, award notice or final decision.
  • Do not include pricing or proposal content in your first message.
  • Have the solicitation number, contract number and modification history available.
  • If a deadline is within five days, call instead of emailing.
Limitation

VISN 2 matters

The firm does not accept matters involving VA facilities in VISN 2 (New York and New Jersey). Matters in other VISNs and before the Corps of Engineers, NAVFAC and civilian agencies are accepted subject to a conflicts check.
